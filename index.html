How It Works

Honesty & Limits

  • Attribution is based on style, not hidden fingerprints.
  • Prompts, editing, translation, and model updates can change style.
  • The profiles are hypotheses supplied for the prototype.
  • The vulgarity rule is a strong heuristic, not proof.
  • Not for academic-integrity or disciplinary decisions.
